Breeze(A)

Basic Rules - Base Conditions:

Viper will always join Cypher on default post wall deployment when default.

Pistol Round - Attack - B site Only

In pistol rounds buy:

  • Full Utility for Astra, Viper & Cypher.

  • Jett - 1 smoke & 1 Ghost

  • Sova - 1 Ghost

Strategy:

  • Sova recons outside the B map back site for whole site.

  • Astra fake smokes entry, smokes right vision, stuns left.

  • Jett dashes site into her smoke

  • Cypher places trips for back-stab, and for mid just after the round starts, and get in position by ‘1:30‘.

  • Clear the positions, and take the site.

  • Sova joins the team immediate after the recon.

  • Cypher places cam on backsite wall to watch mid.

  • Jett has aim backsite.

  • Astra & Viper fallback to main, if no back stab for post plant.

A1 Execute

Strategy:

  • Sova drones A main for any close range combats, and flies drone high, for little bit of site view.

  • Viper joins back team after the wall.

  • Sova recons back site, Astra smokes default. If someone is revealed A cubby, Viper Molly A cubby.

  • Jett dashes in site, into her smoke.

  • Jett will try to fight back site pushing smokes. Cypher pushes further towards mid smoke, with cage

  • Astra stays A main for Post-plant

A2 Execute

Strategy:

  • Viper walls up.

  • Viper, and Cypher shift walk main, while Sova shock darts 1 bounce A Link, and, Jett dashes in immediately while the wall is up.

  • Astra listens for movements in B Site, while team hits A.

  • Sova recons back site on ground, Astra smokes all angles. Viper Molly A cubby.

  • Jett & Sova and Cypher & Viper pinch the opponent in red area, and plant.

  • Astra wraps the enemy from mid,as needed.

A3 Execute

Strategy:

  • Viper uses the wall before round starts

  • Sova recons B site & Viper goes for the Orb Lineup to A.

  • Cypher trips B main, after the round starts.

  • Astra just stays outside A.

  • Jett & Cypher walk up to mid.

  • Ignore Astra smoke here in the screenshot for now.

  • Sova starts walking back to A, from spawn while Astra smokes mid top.

  • Jett peeks site from B mid. Cypher watches mid bottom, for any pressure on Viper. Viper also tries to avoid attracting attention before Sova crosses.

  • If Jett gets a pick we switch to B based on Call, and picks.

  • Else, Viper walls up before my smokes goes away, and Cypher & Jett join Viper to hit mid link A.

  • Sova drones A site, once everyone is mid. Viper opens the orb as soon as drone starts, and Jett dashes immediately, covered by Cypher. While, Viper holds any mid pinch.

  • As soon as drone is over, Sova shock darts A cubby, and Everyone takes site.
