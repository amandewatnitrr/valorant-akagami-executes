# Valorant Executes

This repository contains Valorant executes for attack &amp; defense for all the maps, starting Q1 2026. Subject to revisions in upcoming future.

# **<span class="smallcaps">Team Role distribution</span>**

- Duelist - Kunal is the permanent duelist for the team. Main duelist
  agent roles include - Jett, Waylay. He prefers playing flash initiator
  apart from the duelist role, which mainly includes - Skye & Kayo, can
  also sometimes play reveal initiators. As per the rule-book, he is
  fixed to te Duelist role for the Q1 2026 period.  

- Controller - AkD is the permanent controller for the team. Main
  controller agent roles include - Astra & Omen. As per the rule-book,
  he is fixed to te Controller role for the Q1 2026 period.  

- Reveal Initiator/ Secondary Duelist - Rex is the permanent reveal
  initiator & Secondary Duelist for Q1 2026. Main reveal initiator agent
  roles include - Sova, Fade & Tejo and, Yoru & Neon as Secondary
  Duelists. As, per the rule-book , he is fixed to be the flash
  initiator for the the Q1 2026 period.  

- Flash Initiator/Secondary Controller - Redex is the permanent Flash
  Initiator and Partial Secondary Controller for Q1 2026. Main reveal
  initiator agent roles include - Breach & Skye and Harbour as Secondary
  Controller. As per the rule-book, he is the fixed Flash Initiator role
  for the Q1 2026 period.  

- Primary Sentinel/Secondary Controller - NinjaQT is the Sentinel and,
  Secondary Controller for Q1 2026. His play style matches mainly
  primary controller, but has been assigned to Secondary Controller,
  mainly - Harbour & Viper, and Primary Sentinel - Cypher, Vyse, KJ.  

- Coach/Sub - Nighthowl is the coach for the team. His play style
  matches whatever is missing, and will decide the execute strategies,
  for the team, including role assignment, for the team.
