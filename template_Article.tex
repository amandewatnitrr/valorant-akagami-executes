\item Flash Initiator/Secondary Controller -  Redex is the permanent Flash Initiator and Partial Secondary Controller for Q1 2026. Main reveal initiator agent roles include - Breach \& Skye and Harbour as Secondary Controller. As per the rule-book, he is the fixed Flash Initiator role for the Q1 2026 period. \newline

\item Primary Sentinel/Secondary Controller - NinjaQT is the Sentinel and, Secondary Controller for Q1 2026. His play style matches mainly primary controller, but has been assigned to Secondary Controller, mainly - Harbour \& Viper, and Primary Sentinel - Cypher, Vyse, KJ. \newline

\item Coach/Sub - Nighthowl is the coach for the team. His play style matches whatever is missing, and will decide the execute strategies, for the team, including role assignment, for the team.
